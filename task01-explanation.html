Task 1 explanation

We noticed that the datsaet tracked the car accidents from 2019 to 2025, so we decided to explore temporal patterns it has. To do so, we created a line chart showing the monthly evolution of minor injuries, major injuries, and deaths during this years.

In this plot, we firstly observed that the amount of minor injuries is much greater than the other two overall. The number of deaths is consistently very close to zero, and the number of major injuries is slightly higher but stays quite close as it never goes over 30 for a single month. This means that most accidents didn't cause major damage to the victims.

The chart also reveals interesting general patterns of accidents over time. For one, we noticed that in 2019 there were many more accidents than in the rest of the years, with the total peak being July 2019. There is also a major downfall in the first part of 2020 which can directly correlate to the lockdown that started in March 2020 due to Covid-19. The graph reaches a clear low point in April 2020 and slowly goes back up until stabilizing again in the summer of that year, although it never gets back to the high levels of 2019. Aside from that, in the rest of the plot we notice some low points in the month of August every year, probably due to summer vacations.

We also considered using a streamgraph but since there is such a big difference between one of the variables and the other two, we thought a line plot would be a better fit.
