Task 2 explanation

This visualisation is designed for Pere Capdevila, the head of the General Directorate of Traffic (DGT). It is meant to give an overview of the amount of accidents there have been each month of the year, as well as a comparison for each district, in order to be able to increase the controls for the seasons with the higher amounts, and focus them according to the place where they have the most accidents. It also includes a comparison between last year’s data (in this case, 2025) and the average data since 2019. This way, the results of each year will be able to be compared with the average to see whether the measures have been effective or not. Pere Capdevila has a background in Civil Engineering, and he has been working for the DGT for more than 15 years. He will mainly look at this dashboard from his desktop computer. He is also competent with the use of charts, as he sees them commonly in his day to day. However, this visualisation is meant to be viewed once per year, and Barcelona is only one of the many cities the DGT needs to analyse. Thus, the graphics are kept simple with clear information to be able to make decisions quickly based on them.
